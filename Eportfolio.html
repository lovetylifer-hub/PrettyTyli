SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL · ENTREPRENEURSHIP

Our Entrepreneurship Project

During Senior High School, my group created a small business featuring stickers, button pins, and pain relief oil. What made our project stand out was that we were the only stall that wasn't selling food.

I was involved in creating more button pins and also provided the pain relief oils for our stall. This project helped me develop my creativity, teamwork, and understanding of how a product can stand out in a competitive environment.
